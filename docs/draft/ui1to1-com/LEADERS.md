## Cross-cutting language patterns
- **Single surface:** "single experience" (Microsoft), "single Customer Agent" (Intercom), "single canvas" (Adobe), "connect Google apps with a single tap" (Google).
- **One-to-one:** "1:1 cross-channel personalization" (Salesforce), "one-to-one activation / moments" (Adobe), "uniquely tailored answers" (Google).
- **Unified context:** "one unified profile" (Segment), "real-time customer profiles" (Adobe), "customer history and context" (Intercom), "referencing all your past conversations" (OpenAI).
- **Channel convergence:** "every channel" (Segment, Salesforce, Adobe), "across every customer touchpoint" (Salesforce).
- **Trust/privacy framing:** opt-in connections, explainable sources (Google, OpenAI) — leaders pair personalization with control.
