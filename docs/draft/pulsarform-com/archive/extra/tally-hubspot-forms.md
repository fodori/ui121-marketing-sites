## Summary

HubSpot is treated as the place leads should live, and HubSpot’s own forms as visually and logically limited. Tally builds the form and sends each response into the CRM through Zapier, Make, or a similar tool. The connection is not native. It works on HubSpot Free and Starter, so the team does not upgrade HubSpot just to get logic, multi-page layouts, uploads, or payments. Once the contact is in HubSpot, workflows and property updates run there. HubSpot stays the system of truth. The page says leads can still trigger HubSpot workflows. It does not describe a Tally-side queue or a reply that updates the contact timeline by itself.

## Key facts

- Integration path: Zapier, Make, or similar. Not a native HubSpot connector, on this page.
- Works with HubSpot Free and Starter.
- Features Tally claims HubSpot forms lack: logic, multi-page, uploads, payments.
- CRM remains the source of truth.

## How it works

Submit, middleware maps fields onto a contact, HubSpot workflows fire. The form builder and the CRM are two products with a bridge. Anyone who abandons between them, or who needs a human question, is outside both UIs.

## Implications for pulsarform.com / ui121

CRM sync is mandatory for this buyer, and it is still a handoff. Pulsarform can promise the HubSpot contact and a thread that produced it, so qualification is visible before the workflow email goes out. Do not claim a native HubSpot app unless one exists. Tally is honest that the bridge is Zapier.
