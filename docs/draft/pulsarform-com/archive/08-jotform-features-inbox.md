# Jotform Inbox for submissions

Source: https://www.jotform.com/features/inbox/
Fetched: 2026-09-23
Status: ok

## Summary

The features inbox page is the operator’s filing cabinet for form responses. The promise is search, organize, and manage every submission from any device, with control over how data is viewed and shared. Filters, tags, comments, email reply and forward, PDFs, and a shareable inbox are the feature set. It is free to try.

Organization tools: filter by date, label, tag, submission content, and form fields; color-coded tags; custom tabs that group submissions the way a team wants to open the inbox. Collaboration: share the inbox, comment on a submission, mention a teammate by email, reply to the form-filler by email, or forward the submission. Sharing can be a link with an expiry and edit permissions, or an email invite with read-only or collaborator access.

Data handling: edit a response by reopening the original form and changing answers, download or print, and generate a PDF with the drag-and-drop PDF editor. Related content on the page points at two blog posts about collaborating in Inbox and the product launch of submission organization. The page links out to an FAQ and a tutorial rather than explaining delivery, webhooks, or respondent-side threads.

This is inbox-shaped demand, confirmed. It is still email-out. “Reply” means send an email to the person who filled the form, not continue inside the form they just used.

## Key facts

- Search filters: dates, labels, tags, content, form fields.
- Color tags and custom tabs.
- Comments with email mentions.
- Reply to the filler or forward to a teammate, by email.
- Share links with expiry and permission, or email invites (read-only vs collaborator).
- Edit submissions in the original form; PDF and print.
- Works from any device. Free to try.
- Sibling blogs exist but were not the fetched URL.

## How it works

Submissions land in Inbox. An operator filters or tags them, comments internally, and emails outward. Editing rewrites the stored response through the form UI. Nothing described here updates a live session the respondent still has open, and nothing shows partial progress or drop-off inside the same queue.

## Implications for pulsarform.com / ui121

Jotform has already trained operators to treat submissions like mail: filters, tags, comments, forward. Pulsarform can adopt that vocabulary and then change the reply target. Instead of launching the user’s email client, the reply is an event on the same submission the respondent can see. Internal comments stay; the missing piece is a two-way channel.

Section outline worth mirroring on a product page: find, tag, tab, comment, reply, share, export.
