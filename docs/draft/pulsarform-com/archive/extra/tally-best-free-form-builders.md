## Summary

Marie Martens, prices rechecked 5 August 2026 against vendor pages around 30 July 2026, ranks free builders and warns that free tiers move often. Her tests: what is actually free, whether a non-developer can finish, which integrations are free, and whether the form can match a brand.

Tally leads: Notion-like typing, unlimited forms and responses, 10MB uploads, payments, signatures, piping, logic, and native syncs, with Pro at $29 for branding, CSS, and workspaces. Jotform is the template and integration king, free plan 5 forms, 100 submissions, 10 payments, 10 signed documents, 100MB storage, Bronze $39 for more space. Typeform is the one-question product, free plan capped on submissions and questions, uploads and payments excluded, Salesforce and ad pixels on a business tier she prices at $99, branding removal on Plus at $59. Google Forms is unlimited and Sheet-native, without payments, hidden fields, calculators, redirects, or custom CSS. Feathery aims at product teams: two live forms and 100 submissions on free, SOC 2 there, HIPAA and GDPR on enterprise, signatures and branding removal at $49, a builder that feels like a site editor with a flow map and a desktop/mobile toggle. Later picks in the extract include simpler one-question tools and payment-first builders. The page says no tool wins every column.

## Key facts

- Prices verified by Tally around 30 July 2026, page updated 5 August 2026.

## How it works

Each product is a builder plus a free-plan ceiling. Submissions export to sheets, CRMs, or email. Feathery is the closest to a designed flow with a live device preview. None of the summaries describe an operator replying inside the submission.

## Implications for pulsarform.com / ui121

Use this as a 2026 free-tier cheat sheet, dated, and vendor-biased. The pattern is a race to unlimited responses. Pulsarform’s free story, if any, should not try to out-unlimited Tally. It should say what the free submission becomes: a thread. Feathery’s flow map is a reminder that product teams want to see the path, which an inbox timeline can show better than a builder canvas.
