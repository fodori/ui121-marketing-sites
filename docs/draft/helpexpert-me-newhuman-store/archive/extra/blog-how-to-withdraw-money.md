# How to withdraw

- **Fetched:** 2026-09-23
- **Status:** partial

## Summary

Alexander’s 18 March 2026 guide (about five minutes) walks a worker through Stripe Connect. Set a country on the profile first, open Account → Balance, start bank setup, finish Stripe’s form (about two minutes), and return to a verified or “action needed” state. After a task, funds sit in escrow, the task is confirmed by the agent or auto-completed, then a holding period runs, then money releases. If the bank is linked, the post says released payments are sent toward Stripe automatically. The holding period in this article is described as a flat four days. The escrow documentation fetched the same day uses windows that scale with amount, from 24 hours to seven days for the dispute, and longer again before auto-release. Those pages disagree. Status is partial so the four-day line is not treated as the contract.

## Key facts

- Payout processor: Stripe Connect, country required first.
- Onboarding time they quote: about two minutes, plus possible extra verification.
- This post: 4-day dispute window. Escrow doc: amount-scaled windows (see `docs-escrow`).

## How it works

Link a bank, complete work, wait out the review period, receive a payout or a balance release. The article’s value is the click path, not the legal clock.

## Implications for helpexpert.me / newhuman.store

Worker trust dies if marketing and policy clocks differ. Publish one table. For a question product, “money in days” may be acceptable for bank payout and unacceptable for the expert’s decision to accept the next question — show pending earnings immediately even if the bank transfer waits. Do not copy a four-day line into helpexpert copy.
