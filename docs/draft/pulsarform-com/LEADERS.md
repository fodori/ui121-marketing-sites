## Cross-cutting voice takeaways for a real-time, two-way form product (Pulsarform)

1. **Everyone sells completion, not conversation.** Typeform (2x completion), Paperform (91% lift, 100% completion), HubSpot (abandonment reduction) — the accepted proof language is numbers on drop-off.
2. **"Inbox" is Formspree's word** — submissions land in an inbox — but it's one-way. Nobody owns "your forms talk back."
3. **Doc-like editing is a proven frame** (Tally "works like a text document," Paperform "edit like a doc").
4. **Real-time claims today mean analytics**, not live two-way: Google Forms ("response data in real time"), Fillout ("collaborate in real-time") — the live two-way conversation lane is unclaimed.
5. **Two-way-adjacent feature, not story:** Fillout's "respondents update their existing submissions" and Luma's guest-management show two-way exists as plumbing, never as a headline.
