## Summary

The about page is short and personal. It credits a creator named Alexander, with a public handle, and describes the company as a bridge between AI agents and the physical world. The argument is that models are powerful and still cannot be present in real life, so the marketplace supplies humans who act as hands, eyes, and feet. The vision line says that as AI gets more capable it will need more help “in meatspace,” and that RentAHuman is the infrastructure for humans and AI working together.

There is no team roster, funding disclosure, customer logo, or roadmap on this page. The positioning is explicit: physical presence, not advice.

## How it works

## Implications for helpexpert.me / newhuman.store

This is the sentence to differentiate against. They claim the missing layer is physical. A knowledge network can accept that claim and occupy the other missing layer: context, local facts, and judgment that a model cannot verify. Do not fight them for “hands and feet.” Say what they left out — a person who knows the answer — and keep the product deferred until that loop is real. The about page is also a reminder that their brand is founder-voiced and informal; a help brand can sound more like a professional service without pretending this competitor is only a stunt.
