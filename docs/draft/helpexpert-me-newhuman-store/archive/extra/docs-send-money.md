# RentAHuman docs: send money

- **Source:** https://rentahuman.ai/docs/send-money
- **Fetched:** 2026-09-23
- **Status:** ok
- **Collection:** sibling of https://rentahuman.ai/docs

## Summary

Send Money is an instant transfer inside a conversation: tips, bonuses, reimbursements, or a small payment with no escrow, no dispute, and no delivery confirmation. The docs tell posters to use a bounty with escrow when the task is larger and needs protection. Even inside a funded bounty thread, an extra send is still a direct transfer, sometimes labeled “send additional money.” Legacy direct-message threads are read-only and do not expose the action. The sender opens the details panel, enters $1 to $10,000, and pays through Stripe (cards and wallets Stripe supports in that country). An 18% platform fee is added on top. The recipient gets 100% of the amount entered. A system line in the chat records the transfer. The guide says the flow takes about 30 seconds.

## Key facts

- Range: $1–$10,000 per send.
- Platform fee: 18% added to the amount; recipient receives the entered amount.
- No hold, no dispute, no proof step.
- Requires a bounty or service-booking conversation, not a retired direct thread.
- Rail: Stripe checkout.

## How it works

Pick the thread, enter the amount, pay the fee-inclusive checkout, and both sides see a confirmation message and a history entry. It does not change bounty escrow state.

## Implications for helpexpert.me / newhuman.store

An 18% add-on fee on tips is a concrete price anchor. A per-question product should decide whether the fee sits on top (buyer pays worker amount plus fee) or inside the price, and say so in one sentence the way this page does. Instant send is a bad default for answers that need a quality check, and a good extra for “thank you” after a good answer. Do not make unprotected sends the main rail or the trust story collapses. The product is still deferred; this is a pricing note, not a checkout design.
