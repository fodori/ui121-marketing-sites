## Summary

Written by Marie Martens, last update 5 August 2026. Tally frames Jotform as the long-lived template library and itself as the unlimited document editor. On this page Jotform’s free plan is 5 forms and 100 submissions a month. Paid plans from about $39 to $129 mostly raise those caps. Unlimited forms, unlimited responses, team collaboration, and custom domains are placed on an enterprise tier they price from $59. Tally Pro at $29 claims unlimited teammates and custom domains. Business at $89 adds retention control, which the table marks unavailable on Jotform.

G2 scores they publish: Tally 4.9 versus Jotform 4.7, support 9.3 versus 8.8, logic 9.5 versus 8.8, design 9.2 versus 8.8, payments 8.7 versus 8.0. Jotform advantages they concede include much larger uploads (1GB per submission versus Tally’s 10MB), respondent email and custom-domain sending without Tally’s $29 gate, email verification, analytics pixels, and partial submissions on the free plan. Payments run through about 40 processors, with 10 payment submissions a month on the free plan, then 100, 250, and 1,000 as tiers rise. Custom CSS applies to Classic forms, not Card forms. n8n, Linear, Discord, and a Framer plugin are marked native for Tally and missing or third-party for Jotform. Drop-off insights are Tally Pro only. Both list a free MCP server. Save-and-continue on Jotform, they say, is easy to miss unless multipage autosave is turned on.

## Key facts

- Updated 5 August 2026 by Marie Martens.
- Jotform free, as stated here: 5 forms, 100 submissions, 10 payments.
- Tally Pro $29 with unlimited seats. Jotform teams from enterprise at $59 on this table.
- File size: Jotform 1GB per submission versus Tally 10MB on the free plan.
- About 40 payment processors. Drop-off insights claimed only on Tally.
- MCP marked free on both.

## How it works

Type a Tally form or drag a Jotform template, then push the submission into storage and integrations. Upgrades on Jotform buy volume. Neither column is a two-way inbox. Partial drafts are the closest operational feature, and each vendor describes the other’s version as limited.

## Implications for pulsarform.com / ui121

This comparison shows Jotform winning on operational volume and payments, not on conversation. Pulsarform should not answer with more templates. The feature to absorb is drop-off plus partials, which still are not a shared thread. A live inbox can show the paused question as a case, which neither free plan really does.
