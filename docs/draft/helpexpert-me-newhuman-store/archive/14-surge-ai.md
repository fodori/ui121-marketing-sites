# Surge AI

- **Source:** https://www.surgehq.ai/
- **Fetched:** 2026-09-23
- **Status:** ok

## Summary

Surge’s homepage is a manifesto more than a product sheet. It argues that great human minds were shaped by lived experience, and that data does the same job for AI. The mission line is to “raise AGI” with human qualities — curiosity, wit, imagination, wisdom — rather than optimize models for clicks. A nav label points at frontier data and reinforcement-learning environments sold off the shelf, plus post-training runs, an expert workforce, and enterprise guides.

The page names research artifacts and benchmarks (including a work index, a Hemingway bench, an enterprise bench, a Riemann bench, and others) and routes for enterprise, research, careers, and a software-consultant network. It does not list prices, an API quickstart, or a way for an outside agent to ask one person a question. The fetch is thin: almost all of the words are brand voice and a product menu.

## Key facts

- Category they claim: human intelligence for AGI.
- Offered shapes: off-the-shelf frontier data and RL environments, post-training runs, expert workforce.
- Public benchmarks and research notes are part of the brand.
- No self-serve task poster on this URL.

## How it works

The implied mechanism is a premium human workforce that produces training and evaluation material for labs and enterprises, including packaged post-training runs. Buyers appear to contact the company. Workers appear to join a consultant or expert network. The site does not describe escrow, hourly browse, or MCP on this page.

## Implications for helpexpert.me / newhuman.store

Surge occupies the poetic, high-end “humans make the model wise” slot. That is a different buyer (a lab) and a different deliverable (a dataset or an RL environment) from a real-time help network. Use them as contrast: human intelligence as an ingredient of training, versus human intelligence as a service call. Their expert-workforce nav is a reminder that “experts” in this market often means raters and consultants under contract. A later helpexpert offer should say “available now” so it is not mistaken for another post-training vendor. newhuman.store’s retail tone would clash with this voice; do not imitate the manifesto.
