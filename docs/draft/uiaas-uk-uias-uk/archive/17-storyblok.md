# Storyblok

- Source: https://www.storyblok.com
- Fetched: 2026-09-23
- Status: ok

## Summary

Storyblok’s homepage now leads with content as a system of record for the AI era, not with the visual editor alone. The promise is one governed model: every piece has an owner, a structure search engines and agents can read, and enterprise roles, workflows, and audit trails. A native MCP server and a workflow layer called FlowMotion are how agents are supposed to act on that content without inventing a second source of truth.

Delivery is API-first and headless. The same structured content is sent to whatever frontend the team builds. Framework starters named on the page include Astro, Nuxt, React, Vue, Symfony, and Angular.

## Key facts

- A research claim with FT Longitude: content debt framed as a $4.63 trillion business liability. Treat as Storyblok’s own study, not an independent figure.
- Outcome snippets: 50% faster content operations; 486,000+ pages; 16 global sites in two months; 50% less development time. Customer lines include NRF (40% less development time, a perfect Lighthouse SEO score), Octopus Energy (sites in five minutes, engineer requests down over 65%), Oatly growth, and Virgin Media O2.
- Quotes on the page: Danielle van Zuijlen (TomTom) on creating pages in 10 minutes; Ronan Morris on marketers liking websites again after 100 sites.
- Positioning line: “#1 Headless CMS,” plus an open-source ecosystem and mobile SDKs.

## How it works

Editors work in a visual editor against a defined model. Developers consume the API from their own framework. Agents connect through MCP with the schema and brand voice in view, and FlowMotion keeps those agent tasks inside an approval workflow. The presentation layer stays outside Storyblok. The CMS ships content and structure, not the product chrome.
