## Summary

Wikipedia describes Fiverr as an Israeli global marketplace that connects freelancers with people and businesses that need services. The name comes from the original $5 price on every task. Sellers now charge more, and the article says prices can run into the thousands. It was founded by Micha Kaufman and Shai Wininger, launched on 1 February 2010, is headquartered in Tel Aviv, and listed on the NYSE as FVRR in 2019. The infobox on this fetch lists 2025 revenue of about $431 million and net income of about $21 million, and an employee count of 787 from 2021. Coverage is described as roughly 160 countries. Languages listed include English and several European languages. Registration is required.

History in the article: iOS app in 2013, Android in 2014, a 2015 Amazon suit against more than a thousand sellers accused of fake reviews (Fiverr said it removed violating services), a $60 million Series D the same year led by Square Peg that brought total funding to about $110 million, and 2020 revenue of $189.5 million, up 77% from $107.1 million. In February 2025 the company announced AI features under the name Fiverr Go. Products called out include Fiverr Business (2020) for larger teams and an AI logo maker (2020). Acquisitions noted include VeedMe, AND.CO (later Fiverr Workspace), ClearVoice, SLT Consulting, Working Not Working, CreativeLive, Stoke Talent (about $95 million), AutoDS, and Digis Group. A 2024 nonprofit hub offers credits to registered nonprofits.

Services named in the lead include writing, translation, design, video, and programming. The article does not describe an agent API.

## How it works

Buyers and freelancers meet on a marketplace of listed services. The encyclopedia stresses the two-sided catalog and the company’s expansion into software, education, and agencies via acquisition. It does not document checkout, fees, or automation policy in the portion used here.

## Implications for helpexpert.me / newhuman.store

The $5 origin is the historical proof that micro-priced human work can become a public company. The 2025 revenue scale shows the gig catalog is not a toy. It is also the wrong shape for real-time agent questions: a listing is a pre-packaged service, discovery is search, and the buyer is a person. Fiverr Go means they are adding AI for human users, which is not the same as letting an agent be the buyer. A deferred help brand can cite the $5 precedent for small tickets and should not cite Fiverr as an open API. Any public reuse of these facts needs Wikipedia attribution under CC BY-SA.
