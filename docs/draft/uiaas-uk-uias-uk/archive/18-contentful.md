## Summary

The homepage promise is content that humans trust and agents can read, with personalisation and answer-engine optimisation, scaled across channels, markets, and brands. Contentful is a headless content platform: authors manage structured content in one place; APIs deliver it to sites, apps, and other touchpoints. The frontend is the customer’s.

The page stresses composable content (reuse a component and it updates everywhere), on-brand native AI, and no-code personalisation so marketers can test without a developer.

## Key facts

- Customer metrics on the page: SumUp, 78% higher conversion via personalisation; Mailchimp, 20+ languages; Docusign, 10× content production; release cycles of 5 minutes, down from 2 weeks.
- Platform metrics: 4.6 billion delivery requests across retail on Black Friday 2025; 50% cost reduction through efficiency; 10× go-to-market speed with AI features; 99.99% uptime.
- Ruggable: 25% more conversions and 7× CTR. KFC’s Ashley Travis describes content on kiosks and drive-thru menu boards. Docusign’s Michael Luzmore describes a flexible DXP and AI experiments. A senior creative director describes marketing, product, and engineering sharing one system.
- Support offer: 24/7 support, the same 99.99% uptime SLA, professional services, and a partner ecosystem.

## How it works

Teams model content as reusable pieces, edit in Contentful, and ship the presentation in their own apps. Personalisation rules pick which piece a segment sees. AI drafts in the brand voice; humans remain in the workflow. Integrations cover commerce and other stack tools. The CMS does not render the product UI.
