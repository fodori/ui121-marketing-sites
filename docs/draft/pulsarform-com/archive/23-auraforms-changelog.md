## Summary

Aura Forms publishes a single changelog, not a blog index. Entries from the first stable release through August 2026 describe a conversational business system: forms that take payments, book time, route work, and talk. The page is one scrolling release list, so it was not split into extra notes.

The line this research set was hunting sits in the June 2026 section. Filling out a form becomes a two-way conversation. The form asks questions, and the customer can ask questions back, either by tapping a glowing orb or by speaking in voice mode. Answers cover services, policies, or the form itself. Questions the form cannot answer are saved so the owner can teach it once. Setup is Form Properties, then Questions, plus a business profile. Unanswered items show up there for a human to fill in. Their own rationale: hesitation is when you lose the lead, so the form should answer like a receptionist.

The same June stretch adds resumable forms. Drafts live in the browser and clear after a successful submit. Analytics has an In Progress view: open drafts, average progress, and the question where people paused. Scored quizzes map points or percentages to result screens that can include the person’s own answers. July 2026 adds event ticketing inside the form (tiers, inventory, QR codes, a check-in panel, and voice selection of a tier) and recurring Stripe billing (weekly through annual, optional setup fee, invoices, failed payments, pauses, cancellations) managed in Aura Finance. A blueprint library groups industry journeys into packages.

August 2026, from the top of the page: scheduled SMS, up to two timed messages, with cancelable future sends and operational columns (checkboxes, dropdowns) whose rules can cancel messages, change priority, notify the team, message the customer, or fire a webhook (28 August). Showcase and Configurator pair each section with a visual stage, including voice forms (25 August). The builder becomes a continuous canvas with undo, a section rail, and General, Design, and Logic tabs (21 August). Individual links prefill one recipient and can request a personalized image or short video without changing the public form (11 August). Native Cal.com booking stays inside the form and feeds reminders (8 August). Team workspaces use roles and paid seats (8 August).

Older notes in the same file mention routing that cuts inbox noise by sending each submission only to the people who must act, file uploads inside conversational mode, lower-latency native voices, and a first release built on natural-language form generation plus a basic conversational mode. AuraGlobe is an early voice-reactive visual.

## How it works

A published form can run as a guided or voice conversation. If the visitor asks something off-script, the orb answers from the business profile or files the gap for the owner. Progress can resume from a local draft. Payments, tickets, and Cal.com slots complete inside the same journey and land on the submission record, which can then schedule SMS or call a webhook. The human inbox is implied (submissions, operational columns, team notify) more than specified. The novel piece is the respondent talking back before submit, not an operator replying inside a thread afterward.

## Implications for pulsarform.com / ui121

Aura is a live precedent for “the form answers.” It is a smaller product, and the reply is a knowledge-base orb during completion, plus saved gaps for the owner. Pulsarform can go further: the same two-way channel stays open after submit, as a machine inbox a person and an agent share, not only a receptionist widget on the way in. Resumable drafts plus an in-progress map are the abandonment feature to match. Individual links (one thread per person, public form unchanged) map cleanly onto ui121 tasks.
