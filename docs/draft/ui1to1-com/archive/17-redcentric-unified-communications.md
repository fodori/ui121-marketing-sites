## Summary

Redcentric, a UK managed-communications provider, presents this page as “Engage on every channel.” The customer picks phone, email, webchat, SMS, WhatsApp, or social, and expects the conversation to continue without repetition. Omnichannel engagement is the service that joins interactions, workflow, and customer information. Unified communications is the employee side: voice, video, messaging, and presence so teams stay reachable and answer with context, instead of switching disconnected tools.

The offer is governed, not only connected. They list skills-based routing, priority, callbacks, escalation, and journey-based workflow; AI for triage, summary, translation, self-service, and agent assist where the data and the use case are ready; integration with Teams, Active Directory or Entra ID, CRM, and ITSM; and compliance recording, retention, legal hold, redaction, and audit. PCI-style telephone payments are described as masking card data and keeping it out of recordings. Adding channels without design, they warn, makes handoffs harder to evidence.

Delivery is four steps: discover how customers arrive and where delay sits; design routing, AI, recording, and access; deploy platform, integrations, permissions, and reporting; optimise from reviews and AI insight. A named UK case is West Yorkshire Combined Authority. Fragmented call, email, and webchat systems became a more connected Microsoft Teams environment. Agents move between fewer platforms. Routine changes and recordings no longer wait on another department. Recordings that could take up to three days are available immediately, which speeds complaint work. Scale on the page: 1,000 Teams users, 57 contact-centre agents, 86 concurrent calls, with Power BI reporting. They also cite 29+ years, 24/7 operations, Cisco and Microsoft practice, and a dedicated account manager.

FAQ distinctions: omnichannel is a capability; the goal is less friction plus ownership and control. The same pattern can serve a service desk, not only a large contact centre. AI improves conversations only with guardrails and a human escalation. Security is access control, routing policy, recording, retention, and audit — not an afterthought.

## How it works

Customers move across channels; the operation’s job is to keep context, route by need and skill, and give the employee one working environment. Workflow (callback, self-service, escalation) absorbs wait-time pressure. AI drafts and sorts when the use case is defined; a person still handles what the guardrails mark as human. Identity and business systems (directory, CRM, ITSM) are connected so the agent is not retyping. Recording and audit make the joined-up service defensible in complaints and regulated conversations. The managed-service layer is discovery through ongoing optimisation, which is how they differentiate from “turn on another channel.”
