## How it works

From the profile’s cleaner paragraphs: static analysis and a queue of vetted senior reviewers sit on top of the customer’s existing pull requests. Automation is supposed to rank issues so the human spends time on what matters. The reviewer comments in the host the team already uses. The company, as listed, is not operating.

## Implications for codereview.uk / ui121

PullRequest is the precedent that this category raised real money ($12M) and that “senior humans plus a filter” was the product, not a bot alone. It is also the precedent that the company is marked inactive — so the story cannot be “they won.” codereview.uk can claim the same job with a clearer delivery surface: a ui121 task that is an IDE-like review, not only a GitHub comment from a marketplace. UK reviewer location in their old marketing is a geographic hint, not a proof of demand. Do not repeat Silvercar or HackerOne as case studies unless you verify them elsewhere. The useful line is: review-as-a-service was fundable, the last well-known attempt stopped, and the AI volume shock is the reason to try again.
