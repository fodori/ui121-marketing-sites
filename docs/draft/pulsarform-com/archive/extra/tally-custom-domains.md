## Summary

Tally Pro can serve forms on a subdomain you already own, so the link matches the brand. Tally does not sell domains. You add the DNS records they generate, at a host such as Cloudflare or a registrar, and wait from seconds to hours. A name can point at only one destination. If it already serves a site, verification fails. When DNS resolves, Tally issues a certificate automatically. A failed check shows a failed status. After that, each published form can take a short slug on that host. The same Pro tier is also where they point people who want notification email from their own domain. The page is setup instruction, not a strategy essay.

## Key facts

- Custom domains are a Pro feature.
- Tally does not register domains.
- Certificate is issued after DNS verifies.
- One hostname, one destination.
- Propagation can take hours.
- Form URLs become a chosen slug on that host.
- Custom sending domain is mentioned alongside link branding.

## How it works

Buy a name elsewhere, attach DNS, wait, map a form to a slug. Respondents see your host. Submissions still land in Tally and its integrations. The domain does not create a reply channel.
