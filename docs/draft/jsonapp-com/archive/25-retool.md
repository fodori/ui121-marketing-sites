## Summary

Retool’s homepage, as fetched on 2026-09-23, sells secure internal apps and a new AI app builder, not a JSON format. The headline above the fold is about securing "vibe-coded" apps and trying the app builder. The pitch to enterprises is production readiness without a later security rewrite, one platform that manages many apps rather than a point tool, and business teams building while IT keeps governance. A trust line says 10,000+ teams use it to generate production-ready AI applications.

Customer-scale claims on the page: Ramp saved $8M and 20,000+ hours; a 10x cut in development time across 1,600 studios for supply-chain users; a 10x increase in patients treated in a health example; DoorDash saved $6M and 36,000+ hours; another story of $3M+ profit and 80% faster development. Verticals called out: manufacturers and logistics (warehouses, fulfillment, supply networks) and banks and fintechs that need compliance. The page does not, in the fetched text, explain drag-and-drop, JSON export, or the component model. Those details live in docs and in the comparison articles elsewhere in this archive. Recent blog teasers on the home page are about AI cost, multi-agent governance, and whether you are actually governing AI.

## How it works

From this URL alone: teams generate apps in Retool’s builder, Retool hosts the runtime, and IT is supposed to keep visibility and policy. The page argues that moving fast and passing a security review are the same product, and that the risk of business-built apps is handled by centralized governance. It does not specify the representation of an app.

## Implications for jsonapp.com / ui121

Retool is the incumbent "apps from configuration" product, now talking like an AI app builder with an audit story. A jsonapp.com page should not pretend Retool is only a drag-and-drop toy. The wedge is portability and inspectability: Retool’s speed and governance numbers are the bar, and the counter is that the definition of a ui121 app is JSON you can diff, validate, and render outside one vendor canvas. Use the homepage for the spend and scale claims. Use source 26 for the JSON-config lock-in claim, which this URL does not make.

Do not invent a JSON quote from the homepage. The honest citation is: Retool sells governed AI-built internal apps to large operations teams, with public savings stories in the millions of dollars and tens of thousands of hours.
