## Summary

Tally prices Formstack as enterprise bundling: no free plan, a 14-day trial, and a Forms plan around $83 to $99 a month for one user, 25 forms, and 1,000 submissions per form. Suite is about $250 to $299 for three users, 100 forms, and 5,000 submissions, and that is where templated documents, multi-party e-sign routing, and approval workflows appear. API, SSO, and a success manager are enterprise. HIPAA and native Salesforce, including Experience Cloud, sit on a custom Salesforce plan. G2: Tally 4.9 versus Formstack 4.3 from 471-plus reviews, with price as a common complaint.

Tally’s free plan is the opposite shape: unlimited forms and responses, logic, calculations, 10MB uploads, payments, PDF export, e-sign capture, an AI builder, and MCP. It does not generate templated documents, route a signature across several parties, write natively to Salesforce, or offer HIPAA. Import from Formstack is not one click. You print to PDF or screenshot, use New form → Import, and then rebuild logic by hand, or you paste a description or image into Tally AI. Support claim: a small team answering within about two hours. They attribute the free plan’s survival to being bootstrapped rather than venture-funded.

Who they tell to stay on Formstack: healthcare that needs HIPAA, Salesforce-centric sales ops, teams that must generate documents, and orgs that need SSO and approvals.

## Key facts

- Formstack entry: about $83–$99/month, 1 user, 25 forms, 1,000 submissions per form.
- Suite: about $250–$299, documents and approval workflows.
- HIPAA and native Salesforce: Formstack only, in this comparison.
- Tally: free MCP and AI builder. Formstack: neither, here.
- Migration path: PDF or screenshot import, logic not extracted.
- G2: 4.9 versus 4.3 (471+ Formstack reviews).

## How it works

Formstack is a forms-plus-documents-plus-approvals suite aimed at regulated teams. Tally is a self-serve builder that emits a PDF of a signed submission but does not run a signature route. Submissions still end in storage and integrations. Approval workflows on Formstack are the nearest thing to an inbox, and they are a $250 internal route, not a conversation with the applicant.

## Implications for pulsarform.com / ui121

If Pulsarform ever meets a Formstack buyer, the honest gap is HIPAA, Salesforce, and multi-party documents, not “we have forms too.” The honest wedge is everyone else: a live thread is cheaper than an approval suite when the job is to answer a person. Do not imply ui121 is HIPAA-ready because a competitor comparison mentioned HIPAA.
