# Zirtual

- **Source:** https://www.zirtual.com/
- **Fetched:** 2026-09-23
- **Status:** ok

## Summary

Zirtual sells dedicated US-based virtual assistants on monthly plans. The homepage says assistants, paralegals, outbound agents, and related roles can be matched in as little as one day, with no long-term contract. The pitch is to skip hiring: they recruit, vet, and manage the person so the client delegates. A phone number and a consultation request are the main calls to action.

Service lines include general virtual assistants (described as US-based and college-educated), virtual paralegals for law firms on a fractional subscription, virtual receptionists who answer calls, plus marketing and bookkeeping assistants in the navigation. A highlighted service level is a response within two hours, either with the task done or with an estimated completion time. Plans are month to month and can move up or down. The “how it works” steps are: talk to a specialist, get matched, meet the assistant, and see results within about a week. Testimonials are from a law firm and a startup praising named assistants. There is no public rate card, no browse of available people, and no agent or API language on this page.

## Key facts

- Promise: US-based assistants, matched in about 24 hours.
- Commercial shape: monthly subscription, cancel or resize without a long contract.
- Response claim: within 2 hours, done or an ETA.
- Roles: VA, paralegal, receptionist, marketing, bookkeeping, outbound calling.
- Onboarding: human specialist, then a dedicated person, not a task auction.

## How it works

The client requests a consultation. Zirtual matches someone from a bench they already hired and trained. The client delegates ongoing work to that person. Zirtual remains the employer-side manager. Payment is a plan, not a per-question escrow.

## Implications for helpexpert.me / newhuman.store

Zirtual is the “same human every day” model. It is fast for onboarding (one day) and slow for a new kind of question (you wait for your assistant’s queue, with a two-hour response target). An agent that needs a stranger with a niche fact does not want a monthly retainer. The useful steal is the response-time promise, stated as a number. The thing not to copy is dedication and subscription as the only way to buy human help. A deferred product can sit between Zirtual (one assistant, ongoing) and RentAHuman (one task, any body): one question, a qualified person who is free right now, then done.
