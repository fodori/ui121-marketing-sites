## Summary

Marie Martens, updated 5 August 2026, lists why teams leave SurveyMonkey: the free plan allows 10 questions and 40 responses per survey, paid plans start at $39 a month billed annually, unlimited responses sit on higher tiers, simple surveys feel over-built, and the interface feels dated. She says the tools were tested for time-to-publish, price, design, and ease.

Tally is the unlimited free pick, launched in 2020, Notion-like, with all question types including uploads, payments, and calculations, plus Notion, Airtable, Slack, and Sheets. Pro is $29, or $24 if billed annually, adding domains, partials, and customization. Google Forms is the no-account-for-respondents survey, weak on brand and on logic, fine for internal RSVPs, with Workspace from about $6 per user. Typeform is the conversational survey, free plan 10 responses a month, Basic from $29, Plus from $59, Business from $99 in this table, which again may not match Typeform’s own price page. Jotform, founded 2006 in her text, has 10,000-plus templates, a free cap of 100 submissions and 5 forms, then Bronze $34, Silver $39, and Gold $99. A chat-style NPS tool and an enterprise workflow tool are named in the intro and not fully extracted.

SurveyMonkey remains a research brand. The alternatives are still collection tools. Partials appear only as a Tally Pro bullet.

## Key facts

- SurveyMonkey free, here: 10 questions and 40 responses per survey.
- Paid from $39/month, billed annually.
- Tally founded 2020 in this telling. Pro $24 annual or $29 monthly.
- Typeform prices on this page: $29 / $59 / $99.
- Jotform: since 2006, 10,000+ templates, Bronze $34.
- Google Workspace from about $6 per user.

## How it works

Publish a survey, collect responses under a cap or not, analyze in the tool or a sheet. Chat-style NPS is mentioned as a category and not explained in the extract. No alternative is framed as an ongoing inbox with the respondent.

## Implications for pulsarform.com / ui121

Survey tools optimize a campaign that ends. Pulsarform should not pretend to be SurveyMonkey. The useful contrast is partials and conversational NPS: a score that opens a thread beats a score that waits for a quarterly export. Price numbers are Tally’s August 2026 reading.
