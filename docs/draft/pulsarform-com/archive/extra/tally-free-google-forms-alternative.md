## Summary

Tally treats Google Forms as the free tool that never grows features. Unlimited forms and responses are genuine, and a Google account is required to build. Design is a font and a background. This comparison marks column layout, payments, answer piping, hidden fields, calculations, redirects, custom domains, webhooks, MCP, partial submissions, drop-off insights, and native PDF export as absent. Duplicate prevention works if the respondent signs in. Buying Google Workspace adds storage and human support, not new form powers. Free support is documentation and forums.

Tally’s contrast is its free plan plus payments, logic, and richer embeds such as audio, maps, and Calendly. They cite G2 at 4.9/5 and a July 2026 “Top Trending Survey Software” label, against a category average of 4.41 across 552 products. Google Forms is not reviewed on its own. Workspace as a whole is 4.6 from 45,610 reviews. Setup, use, and support scores for Tally are 9.4, 9.3, and 9.3. Pro is $29 and Business is $89. Google Forms can auto-grade simple quizzes. It cannot embed a scheduler.

## Key facts

- Google Forms: unlimited, free, Google login. No payments or MCP on this table.
- Workspace score 4.6/5 from 45,610 reviews, not form-specific.
- Tally G2 4.9 and a July 2026 trending label. Category average 4.41 across 552 products.
- Duplicate control requires Google sign-in.
- Workspace upgrades add storage and support, not form features, in this account.

## How it works

Google Forms land in a sheet and a simple chart. Tally forms can charge, redirect, or webhook. Both still end at the response. The comparison does not describe anyone replying inside the form.

## Implications for pulsarform.com / ui121

Readers will picture Google Forms first. The upgrade is not a nicer theme. It is turning the sheet row into a thread a machine can answer. Treat Tally’s “no webhooks” cell as their sales table, not as a full survey of Apps Script workarounds.
