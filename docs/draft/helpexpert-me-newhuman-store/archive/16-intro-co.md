## Summary

Intro is a directory for booking time with named operators, founders, and specialists. The fetched page is a long catalog of people and session prices, not an essay. Visible offers cluster in the mid hundreds of dollars per session. Examples on the first screen included $450, $350, $300, $250, $199, $175, $150, and $145. Profiles lean on résumés: a Drybar founder, a Nextdoor co-founder, a Wag founder, a Casamigos sales leader, interior designers featured in shelter magazines, a Hu CEO, a General Assembly co-founder, an Index Ventures partner, and similar names. The headline framing is “book experts and get advice.”

There is no API, MCP, escrow-for-agents, or per-question SKU in the extracted page. The unit of purchase is a session with a famous or credentialed person. Supply is curated and high-status. Demand is a human who wants a conversation, not a software client that needs a fact checked before the next tool call.

## Key facts

- Product: book a session with a listed expert.
- Observed session prices: about $145–$450 on the first portion of the directory.
- Talent shown: founders, investors, designers, operators with exit and press credentials.
- No machine-client surface in this fetch.

## How it works

A visitor browses people, sees a session price and a credibility line, and books. The page does not spell out scheduling, video versus phone, or refunds in the text that was extracted. The commercial shape is clear: high price, low frequency, human buyer.

## Implications for helpexpert.me / newhuman.store

Intro proves people pay hundreds of dollars for an expert’s time, and it prices out the agent use case. An agent that needs a thousand cheap checks cannot buy $300 sessions. The lesson to keep is credential display (who is this person, why trust them). The lesson to reject is the session as the only SKU. A real-time help network can sit under Intro on price and above a gig board on identity: named expertise, small ticket, structured answer, machine client. newhuman.store should not look like a celebrity-advice mall. helpexpert.me can borrow “expert” and must add “available now, by API.”
