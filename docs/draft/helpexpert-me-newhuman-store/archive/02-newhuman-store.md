## Summary

newhuman.store is the same kind of holding page as helpexpert.me: a Squarespace “Coming Soon” screen and nothing else. There is no storefront, catalog, checkout, or statement of what is being sold. The domain reads as a retail metaphor (a store that sells access to a new kind of human) but the site does not say that.

The name collides with unrelated brands that already use “new human” in apparel and fitness. Those collisions were noted in the research brief; this page itself does not mention them, and it does not disclaim them. A trademark search is still required before any public campaign.

## Key facts

- Live page is a coming-soon template only.
- No SKU, price, or “rent a human” claim on the URL itself.
- Name overlap with other “New Human” businesses is a naming risk, not something this page resolves.

## How it works

Nothing is sold. There is no cart, no human listing, and no agent endpoint.

## Implications for helpexpert.me / newhuman.store

“Store” suggests a catalog you can buy from, which fits a marketplace of bookable humans better than a consulting firm. It also sounds consumer and physical, which is the lane RentAHuman already occupies (hands, errands, proof photos). If this domain is used, the copy should say what is stocked: judgment, answers, and availability — not bodies for errands — or the store metaphor will be read as a TaskRabbit clone. Until a product exists, keep the domain in research and do not imply a live shop. Clear the name against shop and fitness uses of “New Human” before spending on brand.
