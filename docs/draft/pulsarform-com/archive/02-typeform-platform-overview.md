# Typeform platform overview

Source: https://www.typeform.com/platform-overview/
Fetched: 2026-09-23
Status: ok

## Summary

The platform page reframes Typeform as more than a form builder. The headline promise is up to 3.5 times more data, then using that data so the next move is faster. Trust is stated as 95% of the Fortune 500. Design arguments are visual polish (colors, logos, custom domains), video questions with recorded answers, and enrichment so you can ask fewer questions and still tailor follow-ups.

“How it works” is a six-step loop a marketer can steal as an outline: design an engaging form (template or AI), connect tools, reach people (link, embed, email, QR), automate qualification and routing, analyze with AI, and share insights (charts, video testimonials, reports). Named connections include HubSpot, Stripe, Calendly, Google Sheets, and Notion, with a claim of 300-plus integrations. The FAQ also lists Salesforce, Mailchimp, Slack, and Shopify, and the structured data on the page still says 120-plus in one feature list, so the public count is inconsistent.

Three AI roles are named. Creator AI builds and brands a form from a description. Interaction AI asks follow-ups so two respondents do not get the same script. Insights AI lets you chat with the results. Workflow Builder plus Contacts and Automations qualify leads, notify teams, send follow-up email or Slack, create tasks, and start onboarding the moment a form completes. The FAQ explicitly contrasts this with exporting a spreadsheet.

Use cases cover lead gen, engagement check-ins, video testimonials, product quizzes, research, hiring, onboarding, and events. Customer lines: WWF Cities on getting patterns back to cities, and SmartBug on a 40% lift that beat their own hypothesis.

Pricing on this page: free plan is unlimited forms and 3,000-plus templates, capped at 10 responses a month. Paid names listed include Basic, Plus, Business, Growth Pro, Talent, and Enterprise, unlocking logic, analytics, branding, and security. The page’s own FAQ says Typeform combines an interactive builder, CRM features, integrations, automations, and AI agents.

## Key facts

- Claim: collect up to 3.5x more data than traditional tools.
- Trusted-by line: 95% of the Fortune 500.
- Free plan: unlimited forms, 3,000+ templates, 10 responses per month.
- AI trio: Creator AI, Interaction AI, Insights AI.
- Integrations cited as both 300+ (body) and 120+ (schema feature list).
- In-form payments (Stripe) and scheduling (Calendly).
- Insights Hub: trends, outliers, sentiment, themes, real-time charts.
- Use-case list spans marketing, product, HR, research, events.

## How it works

Someone starts from a template, a blank canvas, or a prompt. They set questions, logic, and brand, then publish by link, embed, or QR. On submit, automations route the person, ping a team, and push the record into a CRM or sheet. Analysts open Insights Hub or ask Insights AI instead of reading every open-text answer. Video answers can be reused as site testimonials.

Nothing on the page is a shared human queue where an operator replies inside the submission. Acting “in real time” means the workflow fires outward.

## Implications for pulsarform.com / ui121

This is the incumbent platform story Pulsarform has to sit beside: builder plus CRM-ish contacts plus agents plus 300 integrations. The weakness is architectural. Data is designed to leave. A live machine inbox would keep the submission as the object of work — triage, reply, escalate — while still offering the same outward triggers.

Marketing can concede Typeform’s completion and design lead, then point at the FAQ line about acting in real time instead of exporting spreadsheets, and show that Pulsarform’s real time is a thread, not a Zap.

> “These automations help you act on form data in real time instead of manually exporting spreadsheets.” — Typeform platform FAQ, https://www.typeform.com/platform-overview/
