# Routine — One app for tasks, calendar, and notes

- **Source:** https://routine.co/
- **Fetched:** 2026-09-23
- **Status:** ok

## Summary

Routine’s homepage sells a single AI-backed workspace for tasks, meetings, projects, and notes, aimed at founders, freelancers, startups, and SMBs. The claim is “all your work in one place,” with a stated audience of more than 100,000 professionals and teams. The mechanism is capture and delegation: a universal inbox for tasks, messages, and notifications; a desktop hotkey for quick capture; a voice assistant for remembering and organising on the go; meeting notes that become tasks without a meeting bot. A planner and time-blocking sit on one screen, with labels, priorities, projects, and recurrences.

Under that daily layer, Routine describes a data model the customer defines — customers, candidates, bug reports, marketing initiatives — plus databases fed by third-party services, views for dashboards, and workspaces for access control. AI agents and automations are meant to take repetitive work so a person keeps the decision. Testimonials on the page are migration stories from Todoist, Akiflow, Notion, ClickUp, and Asana: less setup than Notion, less constraint than Akiflow, one system of record instead of specialised tools. Integrations are bidirectional so external apps sync into Routine rather than remaining separate inboxes.

The page is a product homepage, not a study. There is no independent usage or time-saved figure beyond the 100k claim and customer quotations.

Section outline: promise; capture features; planning features; custom data model; databases and views; integrations; testimonials by persona (founders, freelancers, startups, SMBs).

## How it works

Work lands in one inbox instead of a notification per SaaS product. Capture creates a task with context attached so the user does not open another app to file it. Meetings are summarised into tasks automatically. Custom objects let a small company retire a specialised tool by modelling that tool’s records inside Routine and syncing the source. AI agents run the repetitive steps; the homepage’s own customer language says the human focuses on decisions. Views are different presentations of the same data, which is a light version of one model, many surfaces.

> “Finally an app that just works in getting things done. I can focus on my work and not worry about which tool to switch to access my data.” — Sarah Chen, Engineering Manager, on the Routine homepage

## Implications for the one-UI domains / ui121

Routine is consumer and SMB proof that people already search for one workspace and will leave Notion or Asana for it. only1ui.com can speak that language without pretending ui121 is a personal planner. The structural rhyme is the custom data model plus views: Routine lets a team define entities and render them as lists and dashboards. ui121’s canvas is that idea as a universal JSON renderer for enterprise tasks — pages, forms, and charts — including tasks an agent creates, not only tasks a person captures with a hotkey. The homepage does not show a governed component catalogue or a backend-agnostic contract, which is the gap the domains can claim.
