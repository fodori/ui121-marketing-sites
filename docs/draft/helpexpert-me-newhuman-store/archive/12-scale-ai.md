# Scale AI

- **Source:** https://www.scale.com/
- **Fetched:** 2026-09-23
- **Status:** ok

## Summary

Scale’s homepage sells reliable AI for high-stakes decisions, with human contributors as the data supply behind frontier models. The visible claim is that leading models run on Scale data, that contributors are recruited with precision, and that 25% of them have advanced degrees. Another line says 90% of the world’s leading generative AI model builders are powered by Scale. A timeline line says the company has been at this for 10 years, since 2016, from autonomous vehicles to frontier models.

Named applications on the page include defense-style intelligence from classified data, a partnership to accelerate Meta’s generative models, clinical summaries that reduce physician load, an interactive journalism experience, real-estate development operations, training data for robotic foundation models, expert-level benchmarks and leaderboards, work with the Center for AI Safety, and agentic AI aimed at private-equity portfolio companies. A “Scale Labs” introduction is teased. The tone is enterprise infrastructure, not a button a script can press to ask one question.

## Key facts

- 25% of contributors described as having advanced degrees.
- 90% of leading generative-model builders claimed as customers of the data.
- Operating since 2016.
- Surfaces: frontier data, evals and leaderboards, vertical deployments (health, government-style intel, robotics, real estate, PE).

## How it works

The page does not publish a self-serve flow. The implied mechanism is a managed data engine: recruit specialized humans, produce training and evaluation data at a bar set by frontier labs, and deploy systems with that data. Engagement looks contractual and project-shaped. There is no public hourly browse, no agent MCP pitch on this homepage fetch, and no per-question checkout.

## Implications for helpexpert.me / newhuman.store

Scale is the proof that labs will pay for human judgment, and the proof that this payment happens as pipelines and degrees, not as a five-minute answer. Do not compete for “the data that trains the model.” Compete for the question a running agent cannot wait weeks to outsource. Their 25% advanced-degree line is a quality bar worth echoing later (who is allowed to answer), without copying their enterprise sales motion. A deferred product can cite Scale as the slow, high-end end of human-in-the-loop and leave the real-time end open.
