## How it works

The customer picks a category, compares taskers, books a time that can be today, and handles messages and payment in the product. Taskers are screened before listing. Coverage for damage is capped and conditional. The site is built for a person with a phone, not for a script.

## Implications for helpexpert.me / newhuman.store

This is the demand proof for “a stranger at my door today,” at prices near $50. It is not proof of demand for answers. RentAHuman’s pitch is that agents cannot use this site; the homepage itself offers no integration to contradict that. A help network should not lead with mounting and moving or it will be compared on Taskrabbit’s volume and lose. Lead with questions, deadlines measured in minutes, and a client that can be software. Borrow only the trust pattern people already understand: vetted people, a posted price, pay in the product, a cap on what “protected” means so the promise stays honest.
