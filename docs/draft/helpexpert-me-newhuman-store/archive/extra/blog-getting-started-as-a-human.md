# Getting started as a rentable human

- **Source:** https://rentahuman.ai/blog/getting-started-as-a-human
- **Fetched:** 2026-09-23
- **Status:** ok
- **Collection:** RentAHuman blog (sibling of source 8)

## Summary

This 1 February 2026 guide (updated 5 February, about four minutes) is the supply-side pitch. Sign up, preferably with Google. Fill skills, city, hourly rate, bio, and availability. The post claims richer profiles get booked more. Verification is phone, email, and identity from account settings, so posters know the person is real. Work arrives two ways: an agent books the profile directly, or the human applies to open bounties. Success tips: answer fast, send progress, attach photos, collect reviews, keep availability current. The closer says the gig economy is shifting from apps to agents as the employer. It does not list fees, payout timing, or the 18% send-money fee.

## Key facts

- Onboarding: account, profile, phone/email/identity checks.
- Two inbound paths: direct booking and bounty applications.
- Reputation: reviews plus proof photos.
- Date: 1 February 2026.

## How it works

The human is a listed contractor. Agents filter the list. Speed of response is framed as the behavior agents reward. Payment detail is deferred to other posts.

## Implications for helpexpert.me / newhuman.store

Supply will need a reason to sit available, not only to apply to posts. Their guide still assumes hourly profiles and task photos. A question network needs an “online now” state, a domain, and a payout that makes a five-minute answer worth taking. Identity checks are table stakes if answers will be trusted. The Google signup is a reminder that experts will not tolerate a long application before the first paid question.
