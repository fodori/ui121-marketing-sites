# Jotform form notifications

Fetched: 2026-09-23
Status: ok

## Summary

Jotform’s notification product is the incumbent definition of “real time” for forms: an alert the moment a response arrives, on a channel the team already watches. The page leads with WhatsApp, SMS, and mobile push, then adds email, Slack, and Microsoft Teams. A demo lets you send yourself a sample alert on a chosen channel before you wire a form.

Customization is about payload, not conversation. You pick which fields appear, from name and contact details to any custom field that matters. Team notifications fan the same event to the right people so quotes and support requests are not missed. Setup is described as one minute from form settings, with no technical skill, and WhatsApp, SMS, Slack, and Teams each enable in a click.

The FAQ defines notifications as alerts sent immediately after submit. Speed is “instantly.” Benefits listed: you do not have to refresh an inbox or dashboard, you answer leads faster, you miss fewer submissions, and a shared Slack, Teams, or WhatsApp group keeps a team aligned. Multiple channels can fire together.

Full channel list in the FAQ: WhatsApp, push, SMS, Slack, Microsoft Teams, Google Chat, RingCentral, SimpleTexting, Twilio, and ClickSend.

What the page never offers is a place to work the submission. The alert tells you something happened. Acting still means opening another tool.

## Key facts

- Channels: WhatsApp, SMS, push, email, Slack, Teams, Google Chat, RingCentral, SimpleTexting, Twilio, ClickSend.
- Field-level control of what the alert contains.
- Team-wide alerts, multiple channels at once.
- Setup claimed as a few clicks from form settings, no code.
- Delivery claim: the moment the submission arrives.
- Explicit benefit: stop checking the dashboard.

## How it works

On submit, Jotform pushes a formatted alert to each enabled channel. The message is a snapshot of chosen fields, not a thread. A person replies in WhatsApp or Slack, outside the form. There is no respondent-facing reply path described here, and no link back into a live form session.

## Implications for pulsarform.com / ui121

This page is the cleanest statement of the status quo Pulsarform replaces. Notifications are a pager. An inbox is a desk. Marketing can acknowledge that instant WhatsApp and SMS are table stakes, then show the submission itself as the object you answer, with the alert as a side effect rather than the product.

A useful contrast line: Jotform’s own benefit is that you no longer live in the dashboard. Pulsarform’s benefit is that the dashboard is where the conversation continues.
