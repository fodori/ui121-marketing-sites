# AI in digital product work (ArcTouch)

- Source: https://www.hygraph.com/blog/ai-in-digital-product-development
- Fetched: 2026-09-23
- Status: ok

## Summary

Luciano, director of AI at ArcTouch (an AKQA and WPP company), writing 12 June 2026, says AI transformation is only useful when it is pinned to four kinds of work: strategy, design, engineering, and QA. ArcTouch has built digital products since 2008, more than 500 of them. The pattern he repeats is that AI takes volume work and specialists keep judgment.

The design section is the one that touches UI delivery. Production chores (specs, resizing) shrink. Figma’s MCP server plus Cursor lets engineers pull tokens and layout into the editor. Generated code is still owned by a human.

## Key facts

- Discovery workshops of 3–5 days. One Fortune 100 session turned a photo of sticky notes into a draft requirements document by day three, then a prototype tested with users, with the build approved in the room.
- Engineering: three technical spikes with AI in the time of one manual spike. Specs implemented in minutes rather than hours via Figma MCP.
- QA: more than 50 scenarios from a codebase in under five minutes; a 35-page visual diff in 20 minutes versus a day, surfacing six errors.

## How it works

AI drafts documents, code, and tests. People direct the tools and review the output. Design and engineering share one source (the Figma file) through a protocol instead of a handoff document. Nothing in the post replaces the design system or the release.

## Implications for uiaas.uk / ui121

Agents are already bridging design files and code. A managed UI is a better landing zone than a generated component the team must then maintain. The ArcTouch rule (the specialist owns every line) is the trust line for any agent that proposes a ui121 screen.
