# Consultations.ai as a Typeform alternative

Source: https://consultations.ai/alternatives/typeform-alternative
Fetched: 2026-09-23
Status: ok

## Summary

Consultations positions itself against Typeform for one job: client intake. Typeform, they say, is excellent at beautiful one-question forms and surveys, and weak at what happens after the last answer. Their product runs an AI intake that qualifies the lead against the firm’s criteria, collects details and documents, books a paid appointment, and hands the professional a brief. Scope is explicit: intake and scheduling, not professional advice. The customer owns the data.

The argument is structural. Branching logic is written in advance. Real people answer vaguely, contradict themselves, or put the important fact in an “anything else” field. A form records that and moves on. A conversation asks. Documents usually fall into a later email chain. A form cannot decline a poor fit, so every unsuitable inquiry still costs reading time. A form also ends at submission, which they call a handoff back to the human, not a finish line. Booking through a separate Calendly link loses anyone who drops between the two tools, and the booking does not know whether the answers qualified.

They concede Typeform wins for surveys, feedback, registration, job applications, research, and quizzes, especially when you will not meet the respondent, and when the questions are fixed and judgment-free. A conversation would add friction there.

Pricing, which they say was read in July 2026 and re-checked for Typeform and IntakeQ on 3 September 2026: Typeform Basic $28 for 100 responses, Plus $56 for 1,000, Business $91 for 10,000, with annual billing at $25 / $50 / $83. Talent $119. Growth Flow $266. HIPAA on Enterprise only. Consultations is a flat $49 to $299 a month per business at any volume. They also table IntakeQ (per practitioner, low-volume caps at 10 and 25 submissions) and Calendly (per seat, no file upload on the tiers they describe). Calendly figures are from the July reading and were not re-verified. The page links sibling alternatives (Captorra, Ruby, Goodcall, Rosie, Jotform, Formstack) that were not fetched.

## Key facts

- Product: AI intake that qualifies, collects documents, books a paid slot, writes a brief, or declines.
- Typeform prices they cite (July 2026, Typeform re-read 3 September 2026): $28 / $56 / $91 monthly for 100 / 1,000 / 10,000 responses.
- HIPAA: Typeform Enterprise only, in their account.
- Consultations price: $49–$299 per business, unmetered inquiries.
- Gap list: pre-authored branches, no unplanned follow-up, documents leave the form, no decline, submit is a handoff.
- Not for high-volume surveys where nobody will be met.

## How it works

The AI talks in plain language, scores the lead against intake criteria, asks for documents during the conversation, books the paid appointment only if the person fits, and produces a brief (timeline, docs, verdict) before the meeting. A poor fit can be declined instead of landing in an inbox. The professional does not read raw responses to decide.

## Implications for pulsarform.com / ui121

This is the sharpest “after the last answer” competitor in the set, aimed at professional services rather than at a general form builder. Pulsarform can share the thesis (submission is a handoff, not a finish) and differ on shape: a form people still recognize, which becomes a live thread a machine can qualify and a human can join, including a brief, instead of replacing the form with a single intake agent. Their decline path is a feature idea: the inbox should be allowed to close a thread without a meeting.

Use their Typeform price table only with the September 2026 date. Do not treat Calendly numbers as fresh.
