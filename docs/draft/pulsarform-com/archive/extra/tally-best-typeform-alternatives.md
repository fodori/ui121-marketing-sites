## Summary

Marie Martens, 5 August 2026, says teams leave Typeform because of submission caps, thin design control, or price, not because the one-question idea failed. She scores alternatives on free limits, ease, features, real monthly cost, integrations, and brand fit.

Her shortlist: Tally for unlimited free responses, multi-page or single-page layouts, media embeds, piping, and calculations, with Pro at $29 for teams, domains, and drop-off, and Business at $89 for verification and deletion. Stripe fees are the only payment commission she mentions. Jotform for 10,000-plus templates, 40-plus payment processors, CRM routing, and approval workflows, with a free plan of 5 forms and 100 submissions, Bronze at $39 for 25 forms and 1,000 submissions, and teams only at enterprise from about $99. Google Forms for zero cost and Sheets, with branching too basic for calculations or piping, and sign-in friction on some controls. Later rows in the extract cover Paperform for design, a research tool for cross-tabs, Formstack for enterprise, ClickUp for turning submissions into tasks (free tier one form, then from $10), and a basic builder capped at 5 forms and 100 entries from $15.

The approval-workflow and task notes are the only post-submit mechanisms, and they live in Jotform or ClickUp, not in Typeform.

## Key facts

- Updated 5 August 2026.
- Switch reasons: caps, design, price.
- Jotform Bronze, here: $39, 25 forms, 1,000 submissions.
- ClickUp mentioned: submissions become tasks, 1 form free, from $10.
- Tally Business $89 for retention and email verification.
- Typeform’s one-question format is treated as a constraint, not the prize.

## How it works

Pick a builder, accept its cap, connect a CRM or a task tool if you need work to happen. ClickUp is the clearest “submission becomes a task” line in the extract. Everything else optimizes collection.

## Implications for pulsarform.com / ui121

ClickUp’s auto-task is a cousin of ui121: the form opens work. Pulsarform can say the task is the form thread itself, not a card in another suite. Approval workflows in Jotform are internal. The respondent is still outside.
