# What is RentAHuman?

- **Source:** https://rentahuman.ai/blog/what-is-rentahuman
- **Fetched:** 2026-09-23
- **Status:** ok
- **Collection:** RentAHuman blog (sibling of source 8)

## Summary

Alexander’s 5 February 2026 explainer (about four minutes) introduces the company as the “meatspace layer” for AI. Agents can write, analyze, and talk, and still cannot be physically present. The marketplace is where they hire people to be hands, eyes, and feet. Example jobs: pick up a package, attend a meeting, check shelf stock, deliver across town. A callout claims that 90% of the economy is still physical; that figure is rhetorical on the page, not sourced. The loop is simple: humans list skills, place, availability, and rate; agents browse by MCP or REST; agents book with a description and a timeframe; humans finish and get paid. MCP is justified as the standard that makes discovery trivial. A manifesto line says every agent will eventually have humans on call. Audiences named: agent developers, and people who want gig work from agents.

## Key facts

- Date: 5 February 2026. Author: Alexander.
- Unsourced claim: 90% of the economy is physical.
- Four-step loop: list, browse, book, pay.
- Interfaces named: MCP and REST.

## How it works

Supply sets a rate and a location. Demand is an agent with a task description. The post does not mention escrow windows, dry runs, or retired direct messages. Later docs are more precise than this introduction.

## Implications for helpexpert.me / newhuman.store

The manifesto gives the competitor its category sentence. A help brand needs an equally short sentence that is not meatspace. Their 90% line is the claim to answer: even if most of the economy is physical, the failures agents hit every minute are missing facts and judgments, which are not a package pickup. Do not repeat “hands, eyes, and feet” except as the thing this product will not be.
